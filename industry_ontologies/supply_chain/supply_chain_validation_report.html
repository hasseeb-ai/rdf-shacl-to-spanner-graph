Polymorphic Logistics Route Navigation:
Trace shipment origins and destinations across diverse facility types
GRAPH SupplyChainGraph MATCH (s:Shipment)-[:ORIGIN]->(orig:Location), (s)-[:DESTINATION]->(dest:Location), (s)-[:SHIPPED_VIA]->(c:Carrier) RETURN s.ShipmentTrackingNumber, orig.LocationCode, dest.LocationCode, c.CarrierName;
Spanner Engine Invariant Verifications:
  • Uniform Label Signatures: The :Location label uniformly exposes (LocationId STRING(36), LocationCode STRING(MAX)) across Warehouses, ManufacturingPlants, and SupplierFacilities. The :Item label uniformly exposes (ItemId STRING(36), UnitCost NUMERIC, QuantityInStock INT64, IsHighValueItem BOOL).
  • Stored Generated Columns: Class equivalence for HighValueItem correctly compiled as IsHighValueItem BOOL AS (UnitCost > 5000.00) STORED in all concrete item tables.
  • Inverse Storage Optimization: ex:hasPart and ex:partOf share physical table ItemParts with transposed source/destination keys, avoiding redundant storage.
  • Constraint Preservation: Cardinality restrictions from SHACL shapes (e.g. minCount 1 on Warehouse.locationCode and FinishedProduct.manufacturedAt) are enforced with physical NOT NULL column constraints.
